Authorisation and regulation

Andrews & Monroe Solicitors is authorised and regulated by the Solicitors Regulation Authority under number 489238. You can check the firm's entry, and the status of any individual solicitor, on the SRA's public register at sra.org.uk.

The firm and its solicitor are bound by the SRA Standards and Regulations, including the SRA Principles and the Code of Conduct for Solicitors and for Firms. Those rules are published in full at sra.org.uk/solicitors/standards-regulations.

SRA digital badge. This is where the SRA's digital badge belongs, and it is currently missing. It has to be obtained from the SRA and embedded — it is deliberately not something that can be reproduced by copying an image, because the point of it is that it links to the firm's verified entry on the register.

VAT

The firm is not VAT registered. No VAT is added to its fees. Disbursements paid on your behalf — court fees, a barrister's fee, and similar — may carry VAT at 20% charged by the supplier, and that is shown separately on your bill.

Equality, diversity and inclusion

The firm operates an equality and diversity policy, for which the Compliance Officer for Legal Practice is responsible. Its commitments in summary:

  • Clients and third parties are treated fairly and with respect, and the service is provided without bias.
  • Recruitment, training and promotion are decided on aptitude and ability alone.
  • No intimidation, victimisation, bullying or harassment is tolerated, from anyone, including clients.
  • No discrimination because of a protected characteristic — age, race, sex, gender reassignment, disability, sexual orientation, religion or belief, pregnancy or maternity, or marriage and civil partnership — nor because of working patterns or trade union membership.
  • Disabled clients. The firm will make reasonable adjustments so that disabled people can use its services. Where an adjustment cannot be made, it will help you find a solicitor who can act instead. Tell the firm what you need — see also the accessibility statement.
