Information, not advice

Everything on this site is general information about areas of law and about the firm. It is not advice, it is not tailored to anybody's circumstances, and it should not be relied on as a substitute for taking advice on your own facts.

The law changes, and pages are not necessarily updated the moment it does. Time limits, figures and procedural descriptions are indicative and must be checked against your own situation before you act on them or decide not to.

The deadline checker and other tools

The employment deadline checker and similar features on this site produce an indication, not an answer. They apply a general rule to a date you supply, and they cannot know about the many things that change the real deadline — early conciliation, continuing acts, claims running to a different limit, or facts you have not entered. Do not rely on a figure produced by this site to decide whether to bring a claim.

No solicitor–client relationship

Sending an enquiry through this site, by email or by telephone does not by itself make you a client. A relationship begins only once the firm has confirmed it is able to act for you, identity and conflict checks are complete, and terms of engagement have been agreed in writing.

Because of that, please keep a first enquiry brief and do not send confidential documents until the firm has confirmed it can act. Information sent before then is still treated as confidential, but the firm may be unable to act if a conflict emerges.

Enquiries by email

The forms on this site do not transmit anything to a server — they open a message in your own email application. Email is not a secure medium, and the firm cannot guarantee the security of anything sent to it by email in transit. If a matter is sensitive, say so and an alternative can be arranged.
